Selected work

Reward Receipt

Independent case study · cashback and rewards clarity in UPI apps

Problem
UPI apps spend heavily on cashback, but users call it bait: they learn they weren't eligible only after paying, and “up to ₹300” can turn out to be ₹0–₹2.
My role
Self-initiated, solo: public research and competitor teardown, problem framing, metrics, and the screen designs.
Decisions
Show an honest eligibility check and a realistic ₹ range before payment, then a receipt, a status trail and one rupee wallet after it, instead of empty scratch cards.
Outcome
A concept, not a shipped feature: 11 designed screens and a proposed north-star metric, Trusted Reward Rate. Built only from public information.

Refund Clock

Independent case study · failed UPI payments and refund status

Problem
A UPI payment fails after the money has left the bank. Users can't tell where it is or when it'll be back, so they retry, call support or file complaints.
My role
Self-initiated, solo: RBI/NPCI rule research, public user complaints, problem framing, metrics and the screen designs.
Decisions
Show where the money is right now, count down to the refund date RBI's T+1/T+5 rules already set, and pre-fill the dispute instead of asking for forms.
Outcome
A concept, not a shipped feature: 4 designed screens and a proposed north-star metric, Calm Resolution Rate. Built only from public information.

Refund Clock Helper

Live AI demo LLM feature · built on the Refund Clock case study

Problem
People describe a stuck UPI payment in their own words (English, Hindi, Hinglish, a pasted bank SMS), and the RBI rules that answer them sit in circulars nobody reads.
My role
Product manager, solo: PRD, a 50-case eval set with quality bars, prompt iterations and the guardrails, then shipping the live demo.
Decisions
The model only extracts facts. Code works out the RBI deadline and escalation, a validator rejects replies that invent days, links or phone numbers or ask for a PIN, and the reply falls back to a safe template.
Outcome
Live, built with Gemini behind a Cloudflare Worker. Evaluated on 50 synthetic cases (20 held out): 100% classification and correct rule, 0% hallucinated rules, 0% unsafe advice. Speed on the free tier is still the open issue.

Problem
Users had no way to view content in the app, and engagement was low.
My role
Owned it end to end: requirements, working with engineering and back-office teams, launch, and follow-up.
Decisions
I built the feature from scratch and introduced subscriptions for different user segments, so each group got a plan that fit it.
Outcome

Magazine tracker

Value Research · internal app

Problem

My role
