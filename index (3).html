Selected work

Media Lounge

Mas Movil super app · content streaming and channel viewing

Problem
Users had no way to view content in the app, and engagement was low.
My role
Owned it end to end: requirements, working with engineering and back-office teams, launch, and follow-up.
Decisions
I built the feature from scratch and introduced subscriptions for different user segments, so each group got a plan that fit it.
Outcome
I set up analytics for the feature on the 1.2M-user super app. In its first month it drove 36,000+ paying users, which puts it on pace for about $400,000 a year.

Offer Basket

Mas Movil super app · offers, separate from Packs and Recharges

Problem
There was no way to give specific users specialized offers.
My role
Owned it end to end, from scoping to release.
What I built
A place for prepaid users to get free and discounted offers, along with a welcome offer for new users.
Outcome
One campaign to a base of 200,000 users drove 38,000 redemptions, about 19% of the base.

Magazine tracker

Value Research · internal app

Problem
Tracking was manual. Someone had to open a different Google Sheet for each month to find a magazine.
My role
I automated it with Apps Script, so one view shows each edition with its tracking ID and courier partner.
Outcome
It replaced the month-by-month lookups and was used internally. It could also have gone live for external users.
